perf(search): pack the picker stage sort key and refresh one colour of king masks

- detail/search_ordering.hpp/.cpp: SearchMovePicker::Candidate now carries a
  single packed std::uint64_t sort_key instead of the separate priority
  tie-break fields. The high half is the offset-binary complement of the
  priority (ascending key == priority descending) and the low half is the
  move tie-break, so ascending std::sort reproduces the old three-key
  comparator exactly. move_tie_break_key is injective for distinct legal
  moves, so the third key (source_index) was unreachable; the counting
  partition and per-stage sort are otherwise unchanged.
- position.cpp: refresh_king_masks now recomputes checkers/pinned for the
  side to move only (the colour after the flip). A full reader inventory
  shows every interpreted read of those masks indexes state.side
  (resolve_move_legality, legal_moves, legal_moves_into_impl,
  has_legal_move); snapshot/restore still copy both arrays, so unmake
  reinstates exact values for the side it restores. The off-turn colour's
  masks are unspecified until it becomes side to move, documented in the
  function comment.
- Position::copy_board_to / GameState::copy_board_to: a single block copy of
  the 64-square board now feeds the SEE exchange contexts
  (detail/static_exchange.hpp), replacing 64 per-square accessor calls.
- Candidate "defer quiet priority computation" from the batch-4 recon was
  dropped: ordering_.priority() reads the live search ordering tables that
  completed child searches mutate before the quiet stage, so deferring it
  would change good/bad-quiet splits and node counts. It is not
  bit-identical and needs a match gate, not a speed gate.

Local verification before the gate: the candidate full build reaches 292/292 targets; the 18-target ctest suite passes 18/18 (exit 0) with TMP/TEMP redirected to build\koi-test-temp.

Speed gate against the isolated 0288b2d baseline: PASS (+6.58% total NPS, +6.57% median row NPS; 5 runs, depth sweep 2..5, 14 noise rows excluded).
