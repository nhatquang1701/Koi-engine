perf(search): fill snapshots in place, scan pins from one ray, and reuse feature slots

- record_snapshot(state, snapshot()) becomes snapshot_into(state, null_move):
  the history slot is filled field by field straight from the state, so
  make/unmake pays no intermediate Snapshot temporary. The full-history
  memmove path, slot selection, and every stored field are unchanged; only
  struct padding in the shifted slot differs and nothing reads it. unapply
  now restores directly from the history slot instead of copying it out first.
- refresh_king_masks fetches each ray word (ray_attacks & occupied) once per
  direction and derives both nearest blockers from that single word; the ray
  is monotone and starts next to the king, so the near end follows the ray's
  step sign. Same pin condition and iteration order.
- FeatureState gains get_or_compute_into (the by-value get_or_compute becomes
  a thin NRVO wrapper) and GameState gains position_features_into; the
  evaluator extractor and the two search fetch sites (ensure_features and
  lmr_parent_features) fill their slots directly, removing one full
  PositionFeatures copy per fetch. Fresh-local sites already elide and were
  left alone.

Candidate 10 (reusing picker-computed quiet-history scores in the negamax
loop) was dropped: the ordering tables mutate during a picker's lifetime
(completed capture subtrees run history updates while the parent picker is
still alive), so a value captured at prepare time is stale for later
candidates and bit-identity is unprovable.
