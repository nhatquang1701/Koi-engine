perf(search): unroll continuation-history divisors and trim check/repetition probes

- Unroll the continuation-history score loops in quiet_history_score and
  continuation_history_score: the runtime division by (distance + 1) becomes
  a sequence of constant-divisor terms, removing idiv from the quiet-priority
  hot path. Same dividends, divisors, slot indices, and accumulation order.
  Write paths left unchanged.
- in_check() for the side to move now reads the maintained checkers mask
  (the same five-term predicate that refresh_king_masks computes and that is
  refreshed after every side flip), with a guard matching is_checked() for a
  kingless state. The other-colour overload keeps the full attack query.
- quiet_move_is_forcing: the moved piece's attack mask is intersected with the
  enemy colours and iterated ascending with countr_zero, replacing the
  64-square scan plus per-piece geometry walks; the occupied == 0 arena
  fallback and the exact visitation order are preserved.
- GameState caches the NativeCheckProbe inputs (side, enemy king, occupied,
  friendly rook/queen and bishop/queen bitboards, king slider rays) in its
  implementation; move_gives_check rebuilds the probe only when one of the six
  inputs differs. The probe is a pure function of those inputs.
- null_move_is_safe drops the provably-false repetition scan (null_move_allowed
  already excludes repetition-sensitive positions at the call site) and uses
  the maintained Position piece count instead of building a TablebaseSnapshot.
- One repetition_count per node now feeds both draw_status and
  is_repetition_sensitive through a combined API; the existing methods
  delegate, so public behavior is unchanged.
